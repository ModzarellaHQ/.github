### 🧀 What is Modzarella?

**Modzarella** is the mod manager for **[Cheese Rolling](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3809440/)**. Download it, flip on the mods you want, press **Play**. It installs the mod loader for you and keeps your mods up to date.

| ⏱️ | **Game Tweaks** | Endless round, freeze bots, slow motion |
| 🧩 | **Core** | Mod menu (F1), free camera and first person |

### 🚀 Get started

1. Download **Modzarella** for your system.
2. Turn on the mods you want and press **Play**.
3. In game, choose **Play Offline** and press **F1** for the mod menu.

Full guide: **[Modzarella README](https://github.com/ModzarellaHQ/Modzarella#readme)**
